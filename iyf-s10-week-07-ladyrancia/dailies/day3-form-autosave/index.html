How Auto-Save Works

  • Form fields are saved to sessionStorage every 5 seconds
  • Data also saves when you switch fields (blur event)
  • On page refresh, saved data is automatically restored
  • Data clears when form is submitted
  • Session storage expires when you close the browser tab
